"Redditstories" is a personal automation tool built and operated solely by its developer to publish the developer's own short-form video content to the developer's own TikTok account. It is not offered as a service to the public and has no other users.

Use

The tool connects only to the developer's own TikTok account via TikTok's official OAuth flow (Login Kit) and posts video files the developer produced locally to that account via the Content Posting API, either as drafts or as direct posts.
